Plan work

Move work into the right day.

Drag work from your list onto the calendar to give it a date. If plans change, simply drag it to a better day. Planning stays quick, so you can get on with the work.

The calendar keeps tasks, events and deadlines in view together, so you can see what a change means for the rest of the week.

Daily view

See the whole day before it begins.

Pick a date and see the tasks, deadlines and events planned for it. Move between those views without losing your place in the calendar.

It is a quick way to answer the practical question at the start of the day: what needs my attention, and what is coming up?

Linked work

Keep every step with the problem it belongs to.

A repair rarely ends with one action. Keep the original problem and the steps that follow it together, so the work stays clear from first report to final check.

When the next update comes in, you can quickly see what has been done and what still needs attention.

Property history

Find out what happened last time.

Each property keeps a history of its work and visits, even after they are finished. Sign in from any of your devices to look back whenever you need to.

Whether a problem returns or someone asks what happened before, the record is there to help you understand the work that was done.

People and context

Find the person and the detail you need.

Keep the people connected to your properties in one searchable contact list. Add the details that matter to you, so the right person is easy to find when you need them.

Add as much or as little detail as you need, with notes that help you remember the context. The next time you need to get in touch, it is all close at hand.
