Coming soon

More ways to stay on top of the work.

My Property Desk is available now. We are planning additions that make it easier to keep evidence, stay ahead of repeat work and act on the messages that come in.

Property records

Photos and documents with the work

Keep photographs, quotes and other files with the property or repair they belong to, so the evidence is there when you need it.

Planning

Reminders for repeat work

Set work to come round again and get a reminder before the next inspection, service or follow-up is due.

Incoming requests

Turn a message into work

Capture a request without retyping everything, then track the next steps beside the relevant property.

Out on a visit

Inspection checklists

Work through a checklist on your phone, add photographs and leave yourself clear follow-up items.

Communication

Connect your email

See relevant conversations and reply from the workspace, with each thread close to the property or repair it concerns.

AI assistance

Help with the details

Ask for a summary, find an earlier answer or review suggested next steps. You stay in control of what gets updated or sent.

These are planned directions, with no release dates yet. We will share more as each feature takes shape.
