Frequently asked questions

A few things you may want to know.

01
What is My Property Desk? +

It is a free online application for organising the practical work involved in managing a small property portfolio. It brings your properties, people, repairs, appointments and dates into one workspace.

02
Who is it for? +

It is designed for one person looking after several properties, whether they own them or manage them for someone else.

03
Is it free to use? +

Yes. You can create an account and use My Property Desk for free.

04
How easy is it to use? +

It is designed to be straightforward. The dashboard brings current work and upcoming dates into view, and you can open each property when you need more detail.

05
How much setup is needed before I can use it? +

Almost none. Create an account and start organising your work. You can add properties, contacts and other details as you go.

06
What devices can I use My Property Desk on? +

You can use it in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is no separate mobile or tablet app to install; sign in to the same account on whichever device you use.

07
Can I see my appointments on a calendar? +

Yes. The dashboard includes a calendar and a view of what you have planned.

08
Can tenants or contractors sign in? +

No. The current application is for the person managing the properties. You can keep tenants, contractors and others as contacts, but they do not get accounts.

09
Are historical records preserved? +

Yes. Previous work stays available, so you can return to a property and see what happened before.

10
How do I report a problem with the application? +

Describe what happened in the report form. You can attach screenshots if they help.
