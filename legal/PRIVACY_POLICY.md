# My Property Desk privacy notice

Effective 8 October 2026.

## Who we are

My Property Desk is operated by Alexander Kireev. For questions about this notice or requests concerning your personal information, use the [Contact page](https://mypropertydesk.co.uk/contact/) or email [support@mypropertydesk.co.uk](mailto:support@mypropertydesk.co.uk).

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** your name, email address and password in hashed form.
- **Workspace information:** details you enter about properties, contacts, issues, tasks, events and notes. This may include personal information about other people, supplied by the account holder.
- **Enquiries and reports:** messages sent through the contact form, any reply address you provide and screenshots attached to problem reports. Attachments are sent with the message and are not stored as workspace files.
- **Technical information:** session and security cookies, and records needed to operate and protect the service, such as IP address, request time and page requested.
- **Public website analytics:** page visits, referring sites, approximate location, browser and device information, and performance measurements from selected public pages. Analytics does not run in the private workspace.

## How we use information

We use account and workspace information to provide the service and manage your account. We use enquiries to respond to you, technical information to maintain security and reliability, and public website analytics to understand and improve the website. Our lawful bases are performance of a contract for account and workspace functions, legitimate interests in support, security and website improvement, and legal obligation where applicable.

## Sharing and international processing

Your workspace is accessible through your account. The operator may access information where needed to provide support, maintain the service or respond to a request. We use service providers for website and database hosting, email delivery and forwarding, the support inbox, and public website analytics. We do not sell personal information or use workspace records for advertising or automated decisions about individuals.

Some providers may process information outside the UK, including in the EEA and the United States. Where UK transfer rules apply, we rely on applicable adequacy arrangements or contractual safeguards. Contact us if you would like more information about a particular transfer.

## Retention and deletion

We keep account and workspace information while your account remains active. Removing an individual workspace item may retain a historical record within your account. Using **Delete account** removes your account and associated workspace records from the active database.

Support messages, delivery records and operational logs may remain for the period needed to handle enquiries, investigate problems, maintain security or meet legal requirements. Copies held by service providers may remain under their retention arrangements after account deletion. Raw public website analytics is retained for up to seven days; aggregated reports remain available for up to six months.

## Cookies and website analytics

We use cookies required for sign-in and request security. Cloudflare Web Analytics measures visits and performance on the home, features, FAQ, coming-soon and privacy pages. It does not run on contact, account or private workspace pages, and it does not use analytics cookies. We use these statistics to improve the public website, not to identify visitors or serve advertising.

You can [turn analytics off or back on](https://mypropertydesk.co.uk/privacy/#cookies-and-analytics) at any time. Turning it off prevents the analytics beacon from loading on subsequent pages in that browser. A preference cookie remembers your choice for one year; clearing cookies may reset it.

## Your rights

You can update your account details and delete your account. Depending on the circumstances, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal information, to receive a copy, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us to make a request. You can complain to the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) if you are unhappy with how a privacy concern is handled.

## Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our use of personal information changes. The effective date above shows when it was last revised.
